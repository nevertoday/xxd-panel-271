## Panel-specific overlay

The transformed design is a small folk-modern brand mark, not a full illustration and not a polished commercial logo. Compress the subject to one identifying contour, pose or structural relation, built from flat color, a few lines and slight handmade irregularity. Keep the mark small inside a large field of uncoated paper. Color is a short warm palette of one to three hues taken from the photograph, printed like spot ink on warm white. Type is a tiny editorial word, number or label at a scale contrast with the mark. Reject a complete scene, realistic illustration, cartoon app icon, smooth vector finish and decorative clutter.

These overlay rules add to `references/soldier-runtime.md`. They never replace the source brief or the family runtime contract.

Write every selected final PNG directly inside one fresh task directory under `~/Desktop/xxd/xxd-panel-271/` (or the explicit `--out` root). Use collision-safe filenames; do not create source, mode, or size subdirectories and do not generate an automatic contact sheet.
